# Welcome to RE3

> **Research Preview** — you are testing an early prototype of RE3, an
> AI-assisted legal research tool for South African case law. Your feedback
> directly shapes what we build next.

RE3 lets you ask legal research questions in plain language and get answers
grounded in real South African judgments — every statement cited, every
citation one click away from the original PDF.

## What's inside right now

| Source | Coverage | Documents |
|---|---|---|
| Western Cape High Court (ZAWCHC) | 2024 | 444 judgments |

More courts are being added — the Constitutional Court (ZACC) and the
Supreme Court of Appeal (ZASCA) are next, followed by core legislation.

*Corpus last updated: 18 July 2026.*

## How to ask good questions

- **Be specific.** "What are the requirements for a PIE eviction of unlawful
  occupiers?" works better than "eviction law".
- **Name what you know** — parties, topic, statute or year all help:
  "Does section 131(6) of the Companies Act suspend liquidation proceedings
  once a business rescue application is filed?"
- **Ask follow-ups.** The chat keeps context, so you can drill into a
  judgment the previous answer cited.
- **Try to break it.** Ask about matters you know well — that is exactly the
  test we need.

Example questions that play to the current corpus:

- *What did the Western Cape High Court decide in 2024 about the eviction of
  the occupiers of central Cape Town, and what conditions were attached?*
- *What factors guide the quantum of damages for unlawful arrest and
  detention?*
- *Which 2024 Western Cape judgments deal with Rule 43 interim maintenance?*

## Citations — how to verify an answer

Every answer cites its sources. Click a citation marker to open the
judgment; hover to preview the cited passage. If a statement has no
citation, treat it with suspicion — and tell us about it.

## Known limitations (please read)

- **Coverage is limited** to the corpus listed above. Judgments from other
  courts or years — and, for now, legislation — are not in the sources; the
  assistant is instructed to say so rather than guess.
- **Lists may be incomplete.** "All judgments about X" answers are based on
  the best-matching passages, not an exhaustive register.
- **AI can make mistakes.** Verify citations before relying on them.
- **RE3 is a research tool, not legal advice.**
